Consent text

Shown in full to the client, who signs by typing their name. Plain text: a line starting with # is a heading, a line starting with - is a bullet point, and a blank line starts a new paragraph. Nothing else is formatted. Changing the text creates a new consent version; earlier signatures keep the version they signed.
